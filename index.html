How to Resize Image Online Free

  1. 1. Upload your JPG, JPEG, or PNG image.

Frequently Asked Questions About Image Resizing

How can I resize an image online for free?

Upload a JPG or PNG image, select the required dimensions and maximum file size, click Resize & Compress Photo, and download the result.

Can I resize an image to 20KB, 50KB or 100KB?

Yes. You can select a maximum file size of 20KB, 50KB or 100KB for the available photo and signature tools.

Can I resize a photo for passport or government forms?

Yes. The tool includes a 35 × 45 mm passport-style option and can compress the output to a selected KB limit. Check the exact requirements of your application before submitting.

Is my image uploaded or stored online?

The image processing is performed locally in your browser. The tool does not need to upload your selected image to a server for resizing.

Why does an online form reject my resized photo?

A form can reject an image if its dimensions, file size, format, background, clarity or other requirements do not match the official instructions. Always check the exact requirements of the application.

How can I reduce image size without losing too much quality?

Start with a clear original, resize to the required dimensions first, and then use the smallest compression needed to meet the required file-size limit.
